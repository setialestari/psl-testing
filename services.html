Our Services

We consistently seek value-adding partners to engage in projects, contracts and procurement across three fields: Construction, Oil & Gas, and Trading.

Sector 01

Construction

Our expertise covers Civil Works for Power Transmission & Distribution Line Construction, Mechanical & Electrical Installation, Infrastructure Works, Maintenance Works, Piling Works & Slope Protection.

Civil Engineering

Civil works for power transmission & distribution line construction, including foundations and erection works.

Telco Towers

Access road construction, civil foundation works and erection works for telecommunication towers.

Piling Works & Slope Protection

Hydraulic and dropped-hammer piling rigs supporting foundation and slope protection works.

Infrastructure Works

Road re-surfacing, drainage works and related civil infrastructure projects.

Mechanical & Electrical Installation

M&E installation works delivered alongside our civil and structural project teams.

Maintenance Works

Ongoing maintenance works across civil and infrastructure assets.

Sector 02

Oil & Gas

EPCC Contractor — HVAC-R & Fire Protection

EPCC Contractor for Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning & Refrigeration (HVAC-R) and Fire Protection Systems. Our experts offer routine maintenance, emergency repairs and complete solutions.

  • Engineering, procurement, construction & commissioning
  • Fire suppression & detection system installation
  • Routine maintenance & emergency repairs

Crane Services

Maintenance & repair, spare parts supply, and load testing & certifications. We specialise in sourcing and providing high-quality, genuine spare parts for a wide range of crane models, with access to extensive inventory that ensures prompt availability and reduces downtime.

  • Maintenance & repair
  • Spare parts supply
  • Load testing & certifications, adhering to industry standards
