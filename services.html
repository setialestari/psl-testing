Our Project Delivery Process

A clear, coordinated process from first enquiry to final handover.

1

Consultation

We understand the requirement, site, scope and project objectives.

2

Planning

We identify the work programme, resources, equipment, materials and technical support required.

3

Procurement

We coordinate materials, equipment and specialist support to meet project requirements.

4

Execution

Our teams carry out the work with appropriate supervision and coordination.

5

Quality & Safety Checks

Work is inspected against specifications, project requirements and applicable safety procedures.

6

Handover

We complete outstanding items and support inspection, testing, documentation and handover.
