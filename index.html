A Sarawak-Based Company Built On Practical Experience

Pembinaan Setia Lestari has developed its capabilities through project work across civil construction, infrastructure, telecommunications, power-related works and oil & gas. //Why Choose PSL

Built Around Practical Delivery

We believe good project delivery starts with understanding the work properly. Our teams coordinate planning, procurement, site activities, quality control and handover with a focus on meeting specifications, programme requirements and applicable safety standards. Where specialist expertise is required, we work with experienced partners and manufacturers to bring the right technical support to the project.

CIDB G7 Contractor

We are a registered CIDB G7 contractor, the highest grade under the CIDB grading system across categories for building, civil engineering, and mechanical & electrical.

UPKJ Class A

We are a registered contractor with the Sarawak Government under UPKJ Class A.

Sarawak-Based Bumiputera Company

Established and headquartered in Kuching, we are locally based and a recognised Sarawak UPKJ Bumiputera company, licensed under MOF, Petronas and Petros.

Quality Management
