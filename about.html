About Pembinaan Setia Lestari

//Who We Are

Who We Are

Pembinaan Setia Lestari is a modest and quietly ambitious Bumiputera Sarawakian Construction and Oil & Gas company. We are comprised of a diverse team of associates and skilled workforce across various disciplines.

Supported by critical Principals and experts in their respective segments, this has allowed us the ability to provide end-to-end services and solutions in the fields of Construction and Oil & Gas — with a Trading arm supplying equipment and spare parts alongside them.

Since 2018, PSL has expanded into several sectors, delivering top quality services across Sarawak, Malaysia — with continuous new technical innovation, customised solutions, and a constant high quality exceeding ISO 9001 standards.
